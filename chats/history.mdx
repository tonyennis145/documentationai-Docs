A Chat is one interaction with Giga. Longer-running continuity is organized around the work itself, especially through [Tracks](/tracks/index), rather than relying on a raw transcript alone.

## Chats and Tracks

A Chat can be linked to **at most one Track**. Giga should never silently choose that Track because Track attachment affects where observations from the Chat can become visible.

When a Track is explicitly selected, the Chat can contribute useful context to that ongoing body of work.

## What carries forward

Giga separates several kinds of continuity:

- **Chat history** keeps the Chat itself.
- **Observations** capture durable things that happened or were learned.
- **Track context** holds standing background and instructions.
- **Track state** captures where the work currently stands.

This separation helps Giga retrieve the right kind of information instead of treating every past message as equally important.

> **For project history, start with the Track**
>
> For decisions, progress, or what changed, Giga should look at the relevant Track and its structured memory before relying on raw files alone.
