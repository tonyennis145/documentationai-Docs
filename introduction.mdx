## How Giga stays grounded

Giga’s product doctrine stays stable, while live workspace details stay dynamic. Current **permissions**, **Tracks**, **Groups**, **Connections**, and available tools are checked from the live workspace instead of being assumed.

> **Live workspace is the source of truth**
>
> If something depends on access, connected tools, or workspace state, Giga checks the live workspace rather than relying on a hard-coded list.
