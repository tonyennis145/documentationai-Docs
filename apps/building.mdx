Building a Mini App is a conversation. There's no builder to learn and no code to write: you describe what you need, and Giga builds it.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the app" icon="message-square">
    Tell Giga what you want, for example: “Build me a tracker for our open deals, with stage, owner and next step.”
  </Step>
  <Step title="Giga builds it" icon="hammer">
    Giga creates the app and, if it needs somewhere to keep information, sets up its [data](/apps/data) too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it in the chat" icon="app-window">
    The app opens right in the conversation so you can use it straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for changes" icon="refresh-cw">
    Want a new column, a different layout or an extra button? Just ask, and Giga updates the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep improving it

Apps aren't finished when they're first built. Come back any time and ask Giga to change them. Giga keeps a history of versions, so earlier versions are never lost.

## Draft, active and archived

New apps start as a **draft** while you try them out. When an app is ready, it becomes **active**. Apps you no longer need can be **archived**.

## Frequently asked questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can I build an app from ChatGPT or Claude?">
    Yes. You can ask Giga to build or change an app from any tool connected through [Giga MCP](/mcp/index), as well as from Giga Workspace.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Will my app match our brand?">
    Yes. Apps automatically use your workspace's design style, and each app can be adjusted if you want something different.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
