## Apps are private to you

A Mini App starts out **private to the person who built it**. Only you can open and change it.

<Callout kind="info">**Coming soon: sharing apps with your team.** You'll be able to share a Mini App with a Group or with specific people, using the same [sharing model](/introduction#users-groups-and-sharing) as everything else in Giga.</Callout>

## Sharing the data

The [data stores](/apps/data) behind your apps can already be shared with people in your workspace, at one of three levels:

| Access | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Read, change and manage who has access |
| **Read and write** | Read and change the data |
| **Read only** | Read the data |

Every data store always has at least one owner.

## Everyone uses their own access

When someone uses an app, it runs **as that person**. They only see the data and tools they already have access to in Giga, so an app never widens anyone's access.
