Most useful apps need somewhere to keep information: the deals in a tracker, the posts in a calendar, the answers to a form. In Giga, that's a **data store**.

## Data stores

A data store is live storage hosted by Giga that your app reads from and writes to.

- **Created for you:** when Giga builds an app that needs data, it sets up a data store at the same time. You can also connect an existing data store to a new app.
- **Live:** changes show up straight away for everyone using the app.
- **Versioned:** Giga keeps a history of changes, so nothing is lost.
- **Reusable:** one data store can power more than one app.

## What else apps can use

Apps can do more than store their own data. By default, an app can:

- **use your [connections](/connect/index)** to read from your other tools
- **read your [Skills](/skills/index)**
- **read and update data stores**

The person who built an app can allow it to use more of Giga's capabilities if it needs them.

## Your access, not someone else's

When someone uses an app, it runs **as that person**, with their own access in Giga. An app never gives anyone access to tools or data they couldn't already use.

## Frequently asked questions

  <Expandable title="Do I need to set up a database?">

  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="Can an app pull in data from our other tools?">
