title: Mini Apps

A **Mini App** is a small, interactive app that lives in your Giga workspace. Think of a deal tracker, a content calendar, a quick approval form, or an editor for something your team works on every day.

## Opens right where you're working

Mini Apps open as an interactive card **inside the conversation**. Just ask Giga to open one:

> “Open the deal tracker.”

This works wherever you use Giga:

- **Giga Workspace**, in the Giga web app
- **ChatGPT** and **Claude**, through [Giga MCP](/mcp/index)

There's nothing to install and no separate website to visit. The app appears in the chat, ready to use.

## Looks like your brand

Mini Apps automatically use your workspace's design style (colors, fonts and the look of buttons and controls), so everything your team builds looks consistent.

## Built in: the Skill Editor

Giga comes with one Mini App ready to go: the **Skill Editor**, which lets you view and edit your team's [Skills](/skills/index) right inside a conversation.
