**Giga MCP** brings your company's Giga into the AI tools your team already uses: Claude, ChatGPT, and any other app that supports MCP (Model Context Protocol). Connect once, and those tools can use your connected apps, your Library and your company's memory, with each person's own Giga permissions.

## Connect

Every tool connects to the same address:

```
https://app.getgiga.com/mcp
```

Sign-in uses Giga's normal OAuth flow, so there's no key to copy. You approve access once, and the tool then acts as you, with exactly your Giga access.

## Memory from ChatGPT and Claude

Giga saves memory when you ask, not after every message.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what to keep" icon="message-square">
    "Save this", "remember that", "@Giga save the decisions from this chat".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Track" icon="folder">
    Giga shows a card asking which Track it belongs to: the conversation's Track first, then the most likely Tracks, plus **Keep it private**. Saving to a Track shares it with everyone on that Track, so Giga never picks for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Giga saves the key points" icon="check-circle">
    Giga sends only the specific statements you want kept, word for word with who said each one, never the whole conversation. It sorts them into decisions, commitments, ideas and so on, links them to what it already knows, and tells you what changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also settle or correct things directly: "decide it: we're dropping the free tier", "Neil shipped that", "that's wrong".

To pick up work, ask Giga to catch you up on a Track. It reads that Track's memory, its open commitments, latest decisions and open questions, before answering.

## Permissions

Giga MCP never widens anyone's access. Every request runs as the signed-in person, using only what they can use in Giga: their Tracks and Groups, their connections and those shared with them, and their Library items.

Access is grouped into scopes, such as memory, skills, workflows, pulses and Slack. You see what you're approving when you connect.
