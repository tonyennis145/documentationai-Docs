## Overview

Giga automates repetitive tasks across your tools and team directly in Slack. You mention `@Giga` followed by a natural language request, and it executes complex workflows like sending invoices, organizing meetings, or updating CRM records. This saves hours weekly by connecting apps such as Google Calendar, Notion, Shopify, QuickBooks, and more.

Key benefits include AI-powered execution, department-specific automations, seamless Slack integration, collaborative team tools, and scalability as your team grows.

## AI-Powered Task Execution

Giga uses advanced AI to interpret your requests and execute multi-step actions. For example, tell it to "organize my meetings today" and it checks your Google Calendar, resolves conflicts, and books rooms.

<Callout kind="info">
  Start with `@Giga` in any Slack channel. Use natural language—no rigid syntax required.
</Callout>

Here are common examples:

<CodeGroup tabs="Meeting,Finance,Sales">
  ```slack
  @Giga organize my meetings today
  ```
  ```slack
  @Giga send this week’s invoices from QuickBooks
  ```
  ```slack
  @Giga draft customer outreach messages for leads in HubSpot
  ```
</CodeGroup>
