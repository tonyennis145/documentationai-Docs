## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A Slack workspace where you have admin or owner permissions
- Access to a web browser for account setup

Giga works in any Slack workspace and integrates with tools like Google Calendar, Notion, Shopify, and more.

</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Setup

Follow these steps to get Giga running in your Slack workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a free account" icon="user-plus">

  Visit [https://app.getgiga.com/signup](https://app.getgiga.com/signup) and sign up with your email or Google account. No credit card required.

  After signup, you'll land on the Giga dashboard where you can manage automations and integrations.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Giga to your Slack workspace" icon="messages-square">

  From the Giga dashboard, click **Add to Slack** and select your workspace.

  Authorize the app in Slack. Giga requires permissions to read messages, post responses, and access linked tools.

  Once added, `@Giga` will appear in your workspace channel list.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure initial settings" icon="settings">

  In Slack, type `/giga setup` to start configuration.

````text
/giga setup
````

  Follow the prompts to connect your first tool, such as Google Calendar or Notion. Enter your API keys if needed (e.g., `YOUR_GOOGLE_API_KEY`).

  Test the connection with `/giga test`.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Send your first command" icon="zap">

  In any Slack channel, mention `@Giga` with a task:

````text
@Giga organize my meetings today
````

  Giga will scan your Google Calendar, summarize events, and post a threaded response with actions.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Giga not responding" icon="alert-triangle">

  - Ensure `@Giga` is added to the channel (use `/invite @Giga`)
  - Check workspace app approvals in Slack settings
  - Restart with `/giga restart`

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Permission errors" icon="lock">

  - Reauthorize Giga in Slack app management
  - Verify tool API keys in Giga dashboard
  - Use `/giga permissions` to review scopes

  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Command failures" icon="x-circle">

  - Test integrations individually: `/giga test calendar`
  - Clear cache: `/giga clear`
  - Contact support via dashboard if issues persist

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
