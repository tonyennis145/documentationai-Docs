title: Recruiting
description: Practical ways recruiting teams can use Giga for role context, candidate research, structured review, interviews, and hiring coordination.

Recruiting work combines role requirements, candidate information, interviews, handoffs, and repeated evaluation methods.

Giga can keep that context organized while helping teams reuse job-related research and review processes across a hiring effort.

## Keep an open role in a Track

Use a [Track](/tracks/index) when a hiring process will continue across multiple candidates, interviews, and decisions.

A role Track can keep:

- durable role background and constraints
- interview plans and files
- meaningful developments
- Current Track State
- linked Chats
- open questions and decisions

This gives the hiring team one durable place for the work around that role.

## Put stable role context in a Context Note

A [Context Note](/tracks/context-notes) is useful for role information that should remain stable across the hiring process.

Examples include:

- what the role is responsible for
- required job-related capabilities
- team context
- durable constraints
- terminology the hiring team uses
- standing instructions for the hiring process

Fast-changing candidate progress belongs in Memory or Current Track State instead.

## Reuse structured review methods with Skills

A [Skill](/skills/index) can capture a repeatable method for job-related recruiting work.

Examples include:

- candidate research
- resume or application review against stated role criteria
- interview-note synthesis
- interview-question preparation
- reference-call summary
- candidate handoff preparation

The Skill can standardize the method and output format across the team.

> **Keep candidate review job-related**
>
> Use the stated role criteria and relevant evidence. Hiring decisions should remain with the people responsible for the process.

## Use a Recruiting Agent for a stable role

A [Recruiting Agent](/agents/index) can provide a consistent AI role across repeated hiring work.

It can carry:

- recruiting-specific instructions
- durable hiring context
- approved Skills
- permitted Connections
- a consistent output structure

For example, the Agent could use a candidate-review Skill and prepare interview context using information the current user is allowed to access.

The live Group and permission model still controls what the Agent can see and use.

## Work with approved recruiting systems

[Connections](/connections/index) can give Giga access to recruiting systems or other approved data sources when they are available in the workspace.

Depending on the live Connection, Giga may be able to work with current information from those systems.

The exact records and actions depend on:

- current user access
- Group sharing
- credential ownership
- provider permissions
- the tools exposed by the Connection

Giga should check the live environment before promising a specific recruiting-system action.

## Standardize interview preparation

A [Workflow](/workflows/index) can help when interview preparation has several repeatable stages.

For example, a **Candidate Interview Prep** Workflow might include:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the role" icon="briefcase">
    Read the durable role context and the job-related criteria that matter for the interview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the candidate evidence" icon="search">
    Gather the permitted candidate information relevant to those criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify questions" icon="help-circle">
    Surface areas that need clarification or deeper evidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the interview brief" icon="file-text">
    Return a concise, structured brief for the interviewer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Workflow keeps the preparation process consistent without replacing human judgment.

## Prepare cleaner interview handoffs

After an interview, Giga can help turn notes into a structured handoff.

A useful handoff can include:

- evidence relevant to the role criteria
- questions answered
- questions still open
- factual follow-up needed
- next steps in the process

Keep observed evidence separate from unsupported inference.

If the hiring process spans several rounds, meaningful changes can stay with the role Track rather than living only in separate Chats.

## Use Pulses for recurring recruiting work

A [Pulse](/pulses/index) can schedule recruiting work that benefits from recurring checks.

Examples include:

- a weekly open-role review
- a recurring hiring-activity summary
- scheduled review of an ongoing role Track
- recurring work through a Recruiting Agent

The Pulse can target an Agent, a Track, or run without a target depending on where the recurring context belongs.

## Keep outward-facing actions deliberate

Giga can help prepare recruiting communication when the relevant context is available.

If a live Connection supports sending a message or making another candidate-facing change, the applicable confirmation rules still apply.

Drafting and reviewing can happen before the outward-facing action is confirmed.
