Marketing work mixes ongoing campaigns, research, content, approvals, and information from several systems.

Giga can keep that work connected to the right context while giving teams reusable ways to research, create, review, and monitor work over time.

## Keep campaigns and initiatives in Tracks

Use a [Track](/tracks/index) when a campaign or marketing initiative will evolve across multiple Chats.

Examples include:

- a product launch
- a content campaign
- a positioning project
- a customer research initiative
- an SEO project
- an ongoing channel experiment

A Track can keep the durable background, decisions, Current Track State, files, and linked Chats around the initiative.

## Reuse research and review methods with Skills

A [Skill](/skills/index) is useful when the team wants the same task method applied repeatedly.

Marketing examples include:

- competitive research
- customer research synthesis
- brand review
- content review
- campaign brief preparation
- meeting or interview summary
- recurring reporting

The Skill stores the reusable method while each Chat supplies the specific subject.

## Create a consistent Marketing Agent

A [Marketing Agent](/agents/index) can provide a stable AI role for repeated marketing work.

The Agent can carry:

- marketing-specific instructions
- durable context
- approved Skills
- permitted Connections
- a consistent way of structuring output

For example, a shared Marketing Agent could apply the team’s content-review Skill and use approved research or analytics Connections when they are available.

The live Group and permission model still determines what that Agent can access.

## Work with current information through Connections

[Connections](/connections/index) can give Giga access to approved marketing systems and data when the workspace supports them.

Depending on the live setup, Giga can use connected information alongside Company Brain and Track context for work such as:

- research
- campaign analysis
- content preparation
- reporting
- checking current information before a review

The exact providers, records, and actions available depend on the current Connection and external-service permissions.

> **Use live data when the work depends on current performance**
>
> Giga should resolve current information from approved Connections rather than treating an old summary or static file as automatically current.

## Standardize campaign work with Workflows

A [Workflow](/workflows/index) fits marketing work with several meaningful stages.

For example, a **Campaign Brief** Workflow might include:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather the context" icon="search">
    Review the audience, product, campaign Track, and relevant approved sources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Develop the brief" icon="file-text">
    Turn the evidence and constraints into a clear campaign direction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review against the standard" icon="check-square">
    Apply the team’s brand or content-review method where the relevant Skill is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the handoff" icon="users">
    Return the approved working brief or clearly flag unresolved questions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Workflow defines the process. Skills can define the task methods used inside individual steps.

## Keep content and context separate

Marketing Tracks often contain both durable context and authored work.

Use the right layer for each:

| Information | Best home |
| --- | --- |
| Stable audience or positioning background | [Context Note](/tracks/context-notes) |
| Meaningful decisions and developments | [Memory](/tracks/memory) |
| Current direction and unresolved questions | Current Track State |
| Drafts, reports, briefs, and deliverables | [Files](/tracks/files) |
| Reusable content or review method | [Skill](/skills/index) |

This keeps the Track useful after the current campaign Chat ends.

## Schedule recurring marketing work with Pulses

A [Pulse](/pulses/index) can run marketing work that genuinely benefits from a schedule.

Examples include:

- a weekly campaign review
- a recurring research check
- a monthly marketing summary
- a scheduled check for meaningful changes around an ongoing Track
- recurring work through a Marketing Agent

The Pulse can target an Agent, a Track, or run without a target.

## Keep publishing actions deliberate

Giga can help research, draft, revise, and prepare marketing work.

When a live Connection supports an outward-facing action, such as posting or sending content, the applicable confirmation rules still apply.

A useful pattern is:

1. research
2. draft
3. review
4. confirm the outward-facing action when required
