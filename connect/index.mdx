**Giga Connect** is how you connect your team's tools to Giga — your CRM, docs, analytics, databases, support desk, and the rest.

The key idea: **connect a tool once, then use it everywhere.** Once a tool is connected to Giga, you can use it from Giga Web and Slack, from your Agents, Workflows and Pulses — and from other AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude through [Giga MCP](/mcp/index), without connecting each app there separately.

```mermaid

    T1[CRM] --> C[Giga Connect]
    T2[Docs & wikis] --> C

    C --> A[Agents, Workflows & Pulses]

    M --> X[ChatGPT, Claude & other AI tools]
```

[Learn how to use your connected tools from other AI tools →](/mcp/index)

## Where to find it in the app

- **Integrations** is where you connect tools: search the catalog and see what's **Connected**.
- **Cloud → Integrations** holds the keys, sign-ins and logins behind your integrations. [Learn about credentials in Cloud →](/connect/vault)

The profile menu's **Use Giga in…** is about the other direction: bringing Giga into Slack, Claude and ChatGPT. See [Giga MCP](/mcp/index).

## What you can connect

Apps from Giga's catalog, your own MCP servers, APIs, PostgreSQL databases, and even websites with no API at all through Giga's cloud browser. If a tool isn't in the catalog, Giga can add it just for your workspace.

[See how to connect your tools →](/connect/connecting)

## How Giga uses your integrations

When you or a teammate works with Giga, it only uses the integrations that person is allowed to use: their own, plus those shared with them.

- **Chats** can use any integration you have access to, and you can @-mention one to point Giga at it.
- **Tracks** can have integrations and databases linked to them.
- [**Agents**](/agents/index) and [**Workflows**](/workflows/index) use integrations as part of their work.
- [**Pulses**](/pulses/index) run as the person who owns them, using that person's integrations.
