title: The Vault
description: Where the keys, sign-ins and logins behind your connections are kept, shared and audited.

The **Vault** is where Giga keeps the credentials behind your connections. Find it in the **Vault** section of the Giga web app.

Secrets in the Vault are encrypted, never sent back to your browser, and never shown to the AI. When Giga uses a connection, the [secure proxy](/connect/security#the-secure-proxy) adds the secret on Giga's servers.

## What's in the Vault

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **API Keys** | Keys and tokens for services that don't use OAuth. Giga attaches them to requests for you, so they're never pasted into a chat. |
| **OAuth Connections** | Accounts you've signed in to with OAuth, like Google or Slack. Giga refreshes them automatically. |
| **Logins** | Usernames and passwords for sites without an API. Agents use them in a browser session. |
| **Databases** | Connection details for your own databases, so agents can query them. |
| **Browser Sessions** | Saved signed-in browser state, so agents can pick up where you left off without logging in again. |
| **Model Providers** | Your own AI model keys, next to the always-available **Giga managed** provider. [Learn more →](/gateway/index#models) |

You see the credentials you added, plus the ones shared with you.

## Adding a credential

Use **New** at the top of the Vault sidebar:

- **Integration:** opens the [Integrations](/connect/connecting) search, where you find an app and connect it.
- **API key, login, database and other custom types:** opens a secure form where you enter the details directly.

## Each credential's page

Open a credential to see its details. If you added it, you can:

- **Rename** it, and change its **handle**, the short name agents use to refer to it
- add a **note** for your team
- **share** it with Groups or specific people
- **link it to Tracks**, so it's at hand when someone works on that Track

People it's shared with can use it, but only the person who added it can change or remove it. [How sharing works →](/connect/sharing)

## Every use is recorded

Each time a credential is used, Giga records who used it, when, and what for (the site or API it called, or a preview of the query). Secrets, request headers and bodies are never recorded.

See the record in **Activity & usage → Credential activity**: your own uses, plus every use of credentials you own. [Learn about Activity & usage →](/teams-and-admin/activity-and-usage)

## Frequently asked questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can anyone see my API key once it's in the Vault?">
    No. Secrets are encrypted and never sent back to the browser, not even to you. People you share a credential with can use it without seeing the secret. Email and password logins are the one exception: they're typed into a website to sign in, so share those only with people you'd trust with the login.
  </Expandable>
  <Expandable title="What happens to the activity record if I delete a credential?">
    The record of past uses is kept, so you can still see what it was used for.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
