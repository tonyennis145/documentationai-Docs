Most teams sign up for a long list of tools: one for AI models, one for data, one for scraping, one for every specialist job. Each one means another subscription, another account and another API key to manage.

**Giga Gateway** lets you skip that. Instead of subscribing to each tool yourself, you use it through Giga.

## Use ours, or bring your own key

For every category, you choose:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use ours" icon="zap">
    Use everything in the catalog through Giga's own accounts, with no separate sign-up and no extra subscriptions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your own key" icon="key">
    Already have an account with a provider? Add your own key and Giga uses that instead.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Either way, everything is available wherever your team uses Giga: in Giga Workspace, in Slack, and in ChatGPT and Claude through [Giga MCP](/mcp/index).

## Why it matters

- **Fewer subscriptions:** stop paying for tools your team only uses occasionally.
- **Less admin:** no new accounts or keys to set up for every tool.
- **One place:** every tool your team uses through Giga is managed in the same place as your [connections](/connect/index).
