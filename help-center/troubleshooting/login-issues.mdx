---
title: Trouble Logging In
description: Fix login problems in Giga.
---

## Issue
- Unable to access your Giga account
- Message: “Invalid email or password”

## Solutions
<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset Password">
    Click "Forgot password?" on the login page and follow prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Email">
    Look for reset email (check spam).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a Different Browser">
    Clear cache or use an incognito window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Support">
    If still stuck, email support@giga.com.
  </Step>
</Steps>
