## Quick Start

1. Go to `https://giga.com/signup` and create your account.
2. Log in to your Giga dashboard.
3. Click "Create New Project".
4. Name your project and click "Start".

## Next Steps
- Use the sidebar to add or manage docs
- Invite your team by email

**Tip:** Giga automates doc structuring—focus on the content, not the setup!
