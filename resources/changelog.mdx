Meaningful product changes, newest first.

<Update label="New app layout, Brain and company memory" description="October 2026" tags={["feature", "change", "removal"]}>

### New features

- **Giga Memory, in Brain.** Giga now remembers your company's work as clear, typed memories: decisions, commitments, ideas, requests, preferences, facts and procedures. Each keeps who said it, a quote and where it came from. Memory stays current: new decisions replace old ones, and commitments close when they're done. [Learn more →](/memory/index)
- **Save to memory from ChatGPT, Claude and Slack.** Say "save this", choose the Track from a card, and Giga saves the key points. Nothing is saved without you choosing where it goes. [Learn more →](/mcp/index#memory-from-chatgpt-and-claude)
- **To file inbox.** Memory saved without a Track stays private to you in **Brain → To file**, with a suggested Track, until you file it or keep it private.
- **Add to memory.** Paste meeting notes or a brain dump into Brain, or import a link such as a Granola meeting. Giga reads it, using the transcript where there is one, and shows progress in a small panel in the bottom-right corner while you keep working.
- **New app layout.** A left rail with Home, Workspace, Vault, Library, Brain, Apps, Integrations, Activity & usage, Settings and Connect, plus **Cmd/Ctrl+K** search. [Learn more →](/workspace/index)
- **Vault.** One place for every credential behind your connections, with sharing and Track links on each one. [Learn more →](/connect/vault)
- **Activity & usage.** Credits and spending, your runs, and a record of every credential use. [Learn more →](/teams-and-admin/activity-and-usage)
- **Library additions.** Each Library item has its own page with access and Track links, and **Bookmarks** are now part of the Library. [Learn more →](/library/index)
- **Instant tools (beta).** Browse thousands of data tools and dozens of AI models your agents can use on Giga's account, and manage them per workspace. [Learn more →](/gateway/index)
- **Model providers.** Bring your own model keys next to the Giga managed provider, in **Vault → Model Providers** or **Integrations → Models**.
- **Team members and Groups** pages in **Workspace**, where admins can edit roles and group membership.

### Changes

- **Settings** has a new sidebar. AI providers moved to **Integrations → Models**, Gateway became **Integrations → Instant**, and Run activity and Credits moved to **Activity & usage**. [Learn more →](/teams-and-admin/workspace-settings)
- Giga no longer saves to memory after every message in ChatGPT and Claude. It saves when you ask.
- Every use of a credential is now recorded, and the record is kept after a credential is deleted.

### Removed

- **State of Play** (Current Track State) is gone from the Track page and from Giga's tools. Ask Giga where a Track stands, and it answers from the Track's memory instead.
- Observation lists on Track pages now show real observations only, not memory system records.

</Update>

## How entries are written

Each entry explains what changed, who it affects, what to do differently if anything, and where to learn more. The changelog reflects shipped product behavior, not plans.
