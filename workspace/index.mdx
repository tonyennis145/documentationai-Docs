**Giga Workspace** is the Giga web app. Anything you'd normally do in ChatGPT or Claude, you can do right here: ask questions, research, write, analyze files and get work done. The difference is that Giga Workspace already has your company's connected tools, shared library and memory built in, so every conversation starts with the context your team needs.

## Use it as much or as little as you like

Giga Workspace is there if you want it, but you don't have to switch. If your team prefers to stay in ChatGPT, Claude or Slack, they can use Giga from those tools instead, with the same connections, library and memory underneath.

## Finding your way around

The rail down the left side of the app takes you to each part of Giga:

| Section | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Start a chat, and pick up recent chats and Tracks |
| **Workspace** | Your team's work: Tracks, Assistants, Skills, Workflows, Pulses, Apps, Team members and Groups |
| **Vault** | The keys, sign-ins and logins behind your connections. [Learn more →](/connect/vault) |
| **Library** | Skills, Context Cards, Saved Prompts, Data Stores and Bookmarks. [Learn more →](/library/index) |
| **Brain** | Your company's memory, and the **To file** inbox. [Learn more →](/memory/index) |
| **Apps** | Your workspace's mini apps. [Learn more →](/apps/index) |
| **Integrations** | Connect your tools, see what's connected, and Instant tools. [Learn more →](/connect/index) |
| **Activity & usage** | Credits and spending, your runs, and credential activity. [Learn more →](/teams-and-admin/activity-and-usage) |
| **Settings** | Your account and workspace settings. [Learn more →](/teams-and-admin/workspace-settings) |
| **Connect** | Bring Giga into Slack, Claude and ChatGPT. [Learn more →](/mcp/index) |

Press **Cmd+K** (or **Ctrl+K**) anywhere to search.

## Background work, in the corner

Some work runs in the background, such as importing a meeting into memory. While it runs, a small panel in the bottom-right corner shows each step as it completes, so you can keep using the app. Click the panel to open or collapse it. When the work finishes, it shows a summary of what happened.
