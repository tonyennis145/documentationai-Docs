A **Context Card** is a short piece of background that Giga adds to every relevant chat: who you are, what your company does, how a Track works, your pricing, your brand voice. Think of it as a briefing Giga always reads first.

Find them in **Library → Context Cards**.

## Who sees a Context Card

Each card has a scope, which decides where it applies and who sees it:

| Scope | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | Everyone in the workspace |
| **Team** | One Group |
| **Track** | Chats and work in one Track |
| **Only you** | Just you |

Use the narrowest scope that fits. Sales-only guidance belongs with the Sales Group, and one project's background belongs with its Track.

## Linking to Tracks

From a card's page you can link it to Tracks, so it's at hand when someone works on them.

## Context Card or memory?

A Context Card is background you write on purpose and keep up to date. [Memory](/memory/index) is what Giga picks up from the work itself: decisions, commitments, open questions. Use a card for "what Giga should always know", and let memory hold "what's happened".
