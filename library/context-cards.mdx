**System Instructions** are short, standing instructions that Giga adds to every relevant chat: who you are, what your company does, your preferences, your pricing, your brand voice. Think of them as a briefing Giga always reads first.

Find them in **Cloud → Shared Resources → System Instructions**.

## Who sees System Instructions

Each one is shared on its own, which decides where it applies and who sees it:

| Shared with | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| **Just you** | Just you |
| **A group** | Everyone in one Group |
| **A track** | Chats and work in one Track |
| **The whole workspace** | Everyone in the workspace |

Use the narrowest scope that fits. Sales-only guidance belongs with the Sales Group, and one project's background belongs with its Track (or in the Track brief, in the Track's **Settings** tab).

## Linking to Tracks

From an instruction's page you can link it to Tracks, so it's at hand when someone works on them.

## System Instructions or memory?

System Instructions are background you write on purpose and keep up to date. [Memory](/memory/index) is what Giga picks up from the work itself: decisions, commitments, open questions. Use System Instructions for "what Giga should always know", and let memory hold "what's happened".
