---
title: Bookmarks
description: Links saved to your Tracks, all in one list.
---

A **Bookmark** is a link saved to a [Track](/tracks/index): a spec, a dashboard, a customer's site, a shared doc. **Library → Bookmarks** shows every bookmark from the Tracks you can access in one list. They're the same links as each Track's **Linked** tab.

## Who sees a Bookmark

A bookmark is visible to the people on its Track. A **private** bookmark is only visible to you.

## Saving a Bookmark

Add a link from a Track's **Linked** tab, or ask Giga to save one ("bookmark this for the Acme Track").
