---
title: Data Stores
description: Live data your apps and agents share.
---

A **Data Store** holds live data that your [apps](/apps/mini-apps) and agents read and update together: a tracker's rows, a dashboard's numbers, a form's responses. Changes sync to every open app instantly.

Find them in **Cloud → Build & Automate → Data Stores**.

## Sharing

A Data Store is shared by granting access to it. Its page shows who has access.
