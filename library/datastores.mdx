---
title: Datastores
description: Live data your mini apps and agents share.
---

A **Datastore** holds live data that your [mini apps](/apps/mini-apps) and agents read and update together: a tracker's rows, a dashboard's numbers, a form's responses. Changes sync to every open app instantly.

Find them in **Library → Data Stores**.

## Sharing

A Datastore is shared by granting access to it. Its page shows who has access.
